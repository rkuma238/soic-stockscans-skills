# Company Analysis Guide

## Key Metrics to Evaluate

### 1. Valuation
- **Stock P/E**: Compare with sector peers and historical range. <15 fair, 15-25 moderate, >25 expensive (varies by sector).
- **Price/Book**: <1.5 undervalued, 1.5-3 fair, >3 expensive.
- **Dividend Yield**: >2% decent for mature companies; check payout sustainability.

### 2. Profitability
- **ROE**: >15% good, >25% excellent. Check consistency over 5-10 years.
- **ROCE**: >15% good, >25% excellent. Should be above cost of capital (~12% in India).
- **OPM%**: Varies by sector. Track trend — rising OPM = improving efficiency.
- **CFO/OP ratio**: >80% means quality earnings (profit converting to cash).

### 3. Growth
- **Sales Growth**: 10Y >10% consistent is strong. Check if growth is accelerating or decelerating.
- **Profit Growth**: Should ideally match or exceed sales growth (operating leverage).
- **Stock Price CAGR**: Should align with earnings growth over long periods.

### 4. Financial Health
- **Debt/Equity**: <0.5 comfortable; >2 leveraged (sector-dependent).
- **Interest Coverage**: >5x safe, <3x concerning.
- **Current Ratio**: >1.5 healthy short-term liquidity.
- **Working Capital Days**: Lower is better; negative working capital is a moat (e.g., FMCG).

### 5. Cash Flow
- **Free Cash Flow**: Consistently positive = good. Growing FCF = compounding machine.
- **CFO vs Net Profit**: CFO should be ≥ 80% of net profit (quality of earnings).

### 6. Shareholding Pattern
- **Promoter holding**: >50% strong, >65% very strong. Increasing promoter stake = confidence.
- **FII/DII trends**: Rising FII = institutional interest. Sudden drops = red flag.
- **Promoter pledging**: Any pledging is a risk signal.

## Red Flags
- Declining sales/profit growth over multiple quarters
- Falling ROE/ROCE trend
- Rising debt or interest costs
- Cash flow not keeping up with profits
- Promoters reducing stake
- High promoter pledging
- Inventory or receivables growing faster than sales

## Green Flags
- Consistent ROE >20% over 5+ years
- Debt-free or reducing debt
- Free cash flow growing in line with profits
- Promoters increasing or maintaining stake
- Dividend payout consistent and sustainable
- Sales and profit growth accelerating

## Analysis Output Format
Structure the analysis as:
1. **Company Snapshot** — what they do, market cap, key metrics
2. **Valuation** — P/E, P/B, dividend yield vs peers
3. **Profitability** — ROE, ROCE, OPM trends
4. **Growth** — Sales/profit growth over 3/5/10 years
5. **Financial Health** — Balance sheet strength, debt, cash flows
6. **Shareholding** — Promoter/FII/DII patterns
7. **Pros & Cons** — From Screener.in's checklist
8. **Verdict** — Overall assessment with caveats
