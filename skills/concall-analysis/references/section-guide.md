# Section Guide — Fund Manager Concall Report

Detailed instructions for each of the 16 report sections.

---

## 1. Executive Summary (Agent1.a + Agent2.b)

**Purpose:** 60-second overview for portfolio review.

- Company, quarter, date
- Headline: "Revenue grew X% YoY to ₹Y Cr" — precise numbers, vs expectations if available
- EBITDA, PAT, margin changes in one paragraph
- 3-5 key themes as bullets
- Management tone: confident/cautious/defensive/evasive — cite specific language
- Forensic credibility score callout (from Agent2)

**Table:** Summary metrics — Revenue, EBITDA, PAT, EPS with Value/YoY/QoQ columns

**Chart:** revenue_trend.html — quarterly revenue bars + YoY% line

---

## 2. Detailed Analysis (Agent1.b)

### 2a. Business Model Evolution
- Revenue mix shifts (product/service, domestic/international)
- Pricing strategy, cost structure changes (fixed vs variable, operating leverage)
- Working capital dynamics, competitive positioning shifts
- **Explain WHY changes matter** — impact on margins, predictability, valuation multiples

### 2b. Industry Operating Environment
- Industry cycle position (early recovery / mid / late / downturn)
- Regulatory tailwinds/headwinds with specifics
- Technology disruptions, supply chain dynamics, input cost trends
- **Connect industry dynamics to this company's specific situation**

### 2c. Management Tone & Sentiment
- Confidence markers ("we will", "we are confident") vs hedging ("we hope", "we expect")
- Evasion signals: tangential responses, excessive caveating
- Tone shifts between prepared remarks and Q&A
- What's NOT being said — topics avoided, superficial answers

### 2d. Key Business Insights
For each (minimum 5-10):
| Insight | Evidence (quote/data) | Significance (H/M/L) | Investment Implication |

### 2e. Qualitative & Quantitative Guidance
| Metric | Guidance | Type (Hard/Soft) | Confidence | Prior Guidance | Change |

### 2f. KPI Deep-Dive
| KPI | Current | YoY Δ | QoQ Δ | Trend | Management Explanation | Trajectory Change Driver |

**Chart:** margin_trend.html — multi-line showing EBITDA%, PAT% over quarters

### 2g. Capital Allocation Strategy
- Capex: maintenance vs growth, timeline, funding
- M&A appetite and pipeline
- Debt management (repayment, refinancing plans)
- Dividends, buybacks, R&D
- **Assess:** Does capital allocation align with stated strategy?

---

## 3. Industry & Company Deep-Dive (Agent1.c)

**THIS IS NOT A SUMMARY.** Provide full, in-depth explanation of every topic discussed.

### Industry-Specific (multiple paragraphs each):
- Market trends: size, growth rate, penetration, TAM
- Competitive landscape: who's gaining/losing share, new entrants, consolidation
- Regulatory/policy: specific policies, implementation timelines, quantified impact
- Economic/geopolitical: trade dynamics, currency, cross-border implications
- Supply chain: bottlenecks, normalization, cost trends

### Company-Specific (multiple paragraphs each):
- Operational challenges and opportunities
- Strategic priorities unique to the company
- Customer demand trends, order flows, client-specific updates
- Management's thought process: how they connect industry dots to strategy

### Interesting Revelations
For each: what was revealed, why it's interesting, investment implication.
**Flag anything you found particularly insightful or helpful** — the fund manager needs this.

---

## 4. Forward-Looking Statements & Guidance (Agent3.a)

### Revenue Expectations
- Specific growth targets or ranges, organic vs inorganic
- Segment breakdown, key assumptions behind guidance

### Margin Expectations
- Gross/EBITDA/EBIT/PAT margin trajectory
- Drivers of expansion or compression, one-time normalizations

### Growth Drivers (ranked by importance + timeline)
| Driver | Type | Timeline | Probability | Revenue Impact |

### PAT Guidance
- Absolute targets, growth rates, tax rate assumptions, below-line items

### Quantitative Guidance Table (every forward-looking number)
| Metric | Current | Forward Guidance | Timeline | Confidence |

### Recovery & Growth Expectations
- Expected timing for acceleration
- Management's confidence level
- What must go right, what could go wrong

## 5. Risk Assessment (Agent2.d) — CRITICAL SECTION

**This is the most important section for the fund manager. Go deep.**

For EACH risk category, provide: description, evidence from call, probability (H/M/L), impact (H/M/L), mitigants mentioned, our assessment of mitigant adequacy.

- **Competitive threats:** specific competitors, share dynamics, pricing pressure
- **Regulatory challenges:** specific regulations, timelines, compliance cost
- **Technology disruption:** AI, automation, platform shifts threatening model
- **Execution risks:** management bandwidth, integration, project delays, cost overruns
- **Market-specific risks:** demand cycles, input costs, currency, geopolitical

**Chart:** risk_matrix.html — scatter plot with probability vs impact per risk

## 6. Peer Comparison (Agent3.b)

- Performance comparison: metrics vs peers
- Growth outlook comparison
- Competition mentions from the call with context
- Peers not mentioned but relevant for benchmarking

## 7. Long-Term Strategy (Agent3.c)

- 3-5 year vision as stated by management
- Alignment with current quarter execution
- Strategic optionality being built or foreclosed
- Investing for future vs managing present — assessment with evidence
- Key strategic bets with risk/reward profiles

## 8. Analyst Q&A (Agent1.d) — EXHAUSTIVE

**Every single question must be covered.**

For each:
| Analyst | Firm | Question | Management Response (detailed) | Evasion Flag | If Evaded: What Was Avoided |

### Recurring Themes
Topics multiple analysts asked about = market concerns. List them.

### Dodged/Partial Answers
| Question | What Was Incomplete | Potential Reason | Implication |

### Follow-Up Questions
What the fund manager should ask in private meetings or next call.

### Margin Analysis from Q&A
Any margin-specific Q&A gets deep commentary on profitability drivers across segments.

## 11. Connecting the Dots (Agent3.d)

**Reads like an analyst's investment note.** Weave data + strategy + judgment:

- How margin guidance connects to capex plans
- Does competitive commentary align with pricing strategy?
- Are growth targets achievable given industry environment?
- Is management tone consistent with reported numbers?
- What story is management telling — does evidence support it?
- Flag any contradictions between different parts of the call

## 13. Forensic Analysis (Agent2 full)

### Earnings Quality Score (/10)
- Cash conversion ratio: OCF/PAT table (5 periods)
  GREEN > 0.8, YELLOW 0.5-0.8, RED < 0.5
- Accrual ratio, revenue quality, receivables divergence

### Management Credibility Score (/10)
| Promised | When | Delivered | Gap % | Verdict |
- Language analysis counts, tone shift analysis, specific evasions

### Red Flag Scanner
| # | Red Flag | Severity (1-10) | Evidence | Implication | Action Required |

12 mandatory checks: revenue recognition, unusual margins, debt covenants, promoter pledges, auditor changes, related-party spikes, capex overruns, working capital bloat, tax anomalies, recurring "one-time" items, inventory write-downs, contingent liabilities

**Chart:** earnings_quality.html — radar chart (cash conversion, accruals, receivables, governance, credibility)
