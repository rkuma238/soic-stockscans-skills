# Global Behavioral Rules

## Mandatory Rule for Equity Analysis: SOIC x StockScans Reports
Whenever performing equity research, stock analysis, or company valuation:
1. You MUST run the `soic-stockscans-fetcher` skill to automatically log into StockScans.in and extract all 6 official SOIC x StockScans reports:
   - **Business Overview**
   - **Growth Catalysts**
   - **Guidance Report**
   - **Forensic Report**
   - **Financial Model**
   - **Deep Dive Report**
2. Incorporate and synthesize all findings from these 6 report cards into the equity analysis report under `### SOIC x StockScans Intelligence & Synthesis`.
3. Explain every technical term, guidance delta, or forensic flag in simple, clear, actionable plain English.
